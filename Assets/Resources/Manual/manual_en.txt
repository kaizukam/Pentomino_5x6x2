■ Selecting a Puzzle

Use the Back (←) and Next (→) buttons.
Tap once to move +1 or -1.
Press and hold for 1 second to fast-forward by ±10 every 0.3 seconds.
After 3 seconds, it speeds up to ±100 every 0.3 seconds.
The minimum is 1, and the maximum is {TOTAL}.

■ Moving Pieces

You can move any unplaced piece.
There are three controls: tap, swipe, and flick.

Tap an unplaced piece to rotate it.
Each tap rotates it 90 degrees counterclockwise.

Swipe and flick
Touch a piece to grab it.
While you hold it, its outline turns red. It goes back to black when you let go.

Swipe by moving your finger while holding the piece to move it.
When the piece enters the grid and approaches a place where it fits, it snaps into position and its outline turns blue.
Release your finger there to place it.
You can place a piece anywhere it fits, even if it is not the correct answer.

Flick by quickly swiping and releasing the piece while holding it.
Normally, a flick would send it off the screen, but here the piece flips where you release it.
Flick horizontally to flip it left to right.
Flick vertically to flip it top to bottom.
The speed of the piece when you release it determines whether the action is treated as a swipe or a flick.
You can adjust this sensitivity in Settings (the gear icon).
The default is 70 mm/s.

■ Using Check

Each puzzle has only one solution,
so the game can immediately tell whether your placed pieces are correct.
If you are unsure, use Check for peace of mind.
If your pieces are correct, there is no penalty.

■ Using Hint

Hint first runs Check and removes all incorrectly placed pieces.
It then works backwards from the last piece of the solution and places the first one still missing.
The penalty equals the number of incorrect pieces plus one hint.

■ About Difficulty Levels

To solve a pentomino puzzle, place pieces in order from the top left.
Fill the current column, then move to the right.

・{GUIDED} — {GUIDED_NOTE}
  Unplaced pieces are arranged in placement order and already face the correct way.
  Ideal for complete beginners or as a young child's first puzzle.

・{TURN} — {TURN_NOTE}
  The order is the same as {GUIDED}, but each piece's orientation is reset.
  You will need to rotate or flip pieces before placing them.

・{CLASSIC} — {CLASSIC_NOTE}
  Pieces are ordered alphabetically by name and have mixed orientations.
  This is the standard pentomino difficulty.

■ Settings

Open Settings with the gear button at the right of the top band.
A long press on the puzzle name opens it too.
